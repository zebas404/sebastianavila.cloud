01 About

I've spent 10 years in IT and the last 5 operating middleware in production: WebLogic, JBoss/WildFly, Apache, Tomcat and IIS, for banks, airlines, rail operators and hospitals in Colombia and Spain, on platforms of 130+ servers.

My cloud journey started at Banco Itaú, where I worked with AWS (EC2, S3, IAM and Elastic Load Balancing) as part of daily operations. Today I'm focused on Cloud Engineering: I hold the OCI Foundations certification and I'm preparing for AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner.

I stay calm when production is on fire, and I like explaining what I learn.
